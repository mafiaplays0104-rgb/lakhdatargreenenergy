Services & Products

Biomass Fuel Solutions for Industrial Boiler Compliance

Overview

Lakhdatar Green Energy manufactures sustainable biomass pellets engineered specifically for thermal plant co-firing, alongside PSL-backed sourcing, logistics, and compliance consulting services that make switching from coal a seamless transition.

Our Product Range

  • Sawdust Biomass Pellets: High-density, low-moisture pellets pressed from surplus sawdust, delivering 3,800–4,200+ kcal/kg GCV for continuous industrial power generation.
  • Mustard Husk Pellets: Agro-residue pellets sourced from local mustard processing, offering a highly cost-efficient coal alternative for high-volume thermal boilers.
  • Agro-Waste Bio Pellets: Blended agricultural residue pellets, engineered for power plants prioritizing mandated co-firing compliance.
  • Industrial Boiler Fuel: Custom-blended pellet formulations calibrated strictly to your specific boiler configuration and thermal requirements.

Our Services

  • Boiler Fuel Optimization: We audit your existing boiler and calibrate pellet blends and feed rates for maximum thermal output.
  • Agro-Pellet Manufacturing: Sawdust, husk, and agro-waste pressed into dense, high-GCV pellets under strict QA protocols.
  • Bulk Supply & Logistics: Custom bulk packaging and high-volume delivery, engineered for an uninterrupted fuel supply chain.
  • Clean Energy Consulting: Guidance on 5-7% mandate transition planning, compliance reporting, and long-term contracts.

Why It Matters

Coal-fired thermal plants face strict 2026 Ministry of Power co-firing mandates, rising costs, and tightening emissions regulations. Biomass pellets solve all three by providing a renewable, locally sourced drop-in fuel that fits existing boiler infrastructure with minimal retrofitting, while actively preventing toxic stubble burning.

Quality & Assurance

Every batch we manufacture is tested to the following strict co-firing standard before dispatch:

  • High GCV: 3,800–4,200+ kcal/kg
  • Low Ash Content: under 10%
  • Low Moisture: under 10%
  • 100% Eco-Friendly Agri-Waste sourcing

Conclusion

Whether securing a single bulk order or a long-term supply contract, Lakhdatar Green Energy pairs the product with the process: pellets engineered for industrial performance, ensuring your transition to biomass is straightforward.
