Services & Products

Fuel and Services Built for Industrial Boilers

Overview

Lakhdatar Green Energy manufactures and supplies biomass pellets engineered specifically for industrial boiler use — alongside the sourcing, logistics, and consulting services that make switching from coal a low-friction decision, not a leap of faith.

Our Product Range

  • Sawdust Biomass Pellets — High-density, low-moisture pellets pressed from wood sawdust, delivering consistent calorific output for continuous industrial use.
  • Mustard Husk Pellets — Agro-residue pellets sourced from mustard processing, offering a cost-efficient coal alternative for high-volume boilers.
  • Agro-Waste Bio Pellets — Blended agricultural residue pellets, engineered for facilities prioritizing zero-waste sourcing.
  • Industrial Boiler Fuel — Custom-blended pellet formulations calibrated to your specific boiler configuration and heat requirement.

Our Services

  • Boiler Fuel Optimization — We audit your existing boiler and calibrate pellet blends and feed rates for maximum thermal output.
  • Agro-Pellet Manufacturing — Sawdust, husk, and agro-waste pressed into dense, high-GCV pellets under strict QA protocols.
  • Bulk Supply & Logistics — Custom bulk packaging and high-volume delivery, engineered for an uninterrupted fuel supply chain.
  • Clean Energy Consulting — Guidance on transition planning, compliance reporting, and long-term supply contracts.

Why It Matters

Industrial boilers running on coal face rising costs, tightening emissions regulations, and unpredictable supply. Biomass pellets solve for all three — a renewable, locally sourced fuel that fits into existing boiler infrastructure with minimal retrofitting, while cutting particulate output and supporting the farming communities the raw material comes from.

Quality & Assurance

Every batch we manufacture is tested to the following standard before it ships:

  • High GCV: 3,800–4,200+ kcal/kg
  • Low Ash Content: under 10%
  • Low Moisture: under 10%
  • 100% Eco-Friendly Agri-Waste sourcing

Conclusion

Whether you need a single bulk order or a long-term supply contract, Lakhdatar Green Energy pairs the product with the process — pellets engineered for performance, and a service team that makes the transition to biomass straightforward from first quote to ongoing delivery.
