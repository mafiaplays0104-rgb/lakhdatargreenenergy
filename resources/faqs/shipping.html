SHIPPING & LOGISTICS FAQS

Bulk Biomass Transport & Delivery

We operate a robust pan-India supply chain. From our primary manufacturing facility in Tilda, Chhattisgarh, we deliver bulk high-GCV biomass pellets to thermal power plants (TPPs), textile mills, and manufacturing hubs across Chhattisgarh, Maharashtra, Gujarat, Odisha, Madhya Pradesh, and neighboring states.

For standard industrial delivery logistics, our Minimum Order Quantity (MOQ) starts at 20 metric tonnes (equivalent to a standard full truckload). However, for utility-scale thermal power generation and GENCOs, we execute multi-thousand-tonne monthly contracts tailored to your specific 5-7% co-firing mandates.

Maintaining strict <10% moisture content is critical. For mid-scale industrial boilers, we supply 50kg moisture-resistant HDPE bags securely palletized. For high-volume utility clients and TPPs, we utilize 1-tonne jumbo bags (FIBCs) with specialized moisture barriers or arrange bulk pneumatic truck deliveries for direct silo injection.

Absolutely. As a registered vendor on the SAMARTH and GeM portals, we have engineered our logistics infrastructure to handle the high-volume, continuous supply chains required by major thermal power plants to comply with Ministry of Power coal-displacement regulations.

For clients within our primary central and eastern India service regions, standard delivery timelines range from 5 to 7 business days. For clients under Long-Term Supply Agreements (LTSAs), we utilize predictive dispatch modeling to ensure just-in-time (JIT) delivery, guaranteeing zero plant downtime.

Yes. We highly recommend structuring bulk supply agreements (MOUs) with custom recurring delivery schedules aligned to your consumption rate. This locks in your fuel pricing against market volatility and ensures an uninterrupted supply chain perfectly synchronized with your daily boiler consumption.

We utilize a highly efficient multimodal transport strategy. For regional deliveries and localized industrial hubs, we deploy a fleet of high-capacity bulk trucks. For massive interstate utility contracts, we leverage India's rail freight network directly from our Chhattisgarh hub to maximize cost-efficiency and lower transport emissions.

We work closely with your plant engineers to align our delivery mechanics with your existing infrastructure. Whether your facility requires forklift-assisted jumbo bag offloading or pneumatic discharge directly into biomass storage silos, we customize our logistics to ensure seamless, dust-free fuel integration.
