International Export & Shipping FAQs

Yes, we are actively expanding into export markets to meet global industrial demand. As heavy industries worldwide transition away from coal, our specialized sales team handles comprehensive international bulk supply inquiries to ensure seamless cross-border logistics and international supply chain management.

We strictly comply with international biomass fuel shipping standards for industrial applications. For all export consignments, our logistics team provides the necessary documentation, including verified phytosanitary certificates as required, to ensure zero pathogen transmission and safe customs clearance.

Protecting biomass from oceanic humidity is critical, which is why we utilize export-grade packaging equipped with advanced moisture barriers. Our bulk supplies are securely palletized for safe and efficient container loading, ensuring the fuel arrives with strict <10% moisture content.

We are strategically positioned to meet global thermal energy demand, actively exploring and serving markets across Southeast Asia, the Middle East, and East Africa. Please contact our export division to confirm specific country availability, FOB/CIF terms, and tailored maritime freight timelines.
