Decarbonizing Industrial Heat: 2026 Outlook

Lakhdatar Research 15 min readMay 2026

Abstract

This whitepaper examines the technical and economic feasibility of replacing coal with biomass pellets in industrial thermal applications across India. We analyze current adoption rates, policy incentives, and project a 40% market penetration of biomass fuels in select manufacturing sectors by 2030.

Key Findings

  • Biomass pellets can replace up to 97% of coal consumption in compatible boiler systems.
  • Net fuel cost savings of 12–18% when factoring in carbon credit monetization.
  • SOx/NOx emission reduction of 60–80% compared to coal combustion.
  • Average payback period for fuel switching: 8–14 months.

Market Landscape

India's biomass pellet production capacity is projected to exceed 50 million tonnes annually by 2028, driven by government mandates for co-firing and direct industrial adoption.

Policy Drivers

The National Biomass Mission, state-level renewable energy certificates, and tightening emission norms under the National Clean Air Programme are accelerating the coal-to-biomass transition.
