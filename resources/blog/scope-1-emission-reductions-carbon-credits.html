Scope 1 Emission Reductions & Carbon Credits via Biomass Pellets

Lakhdatar Editorial 7 min readMay 2026

Decarbonizing Industrial Heat and Capitalizing on CCTS

Decarbonizing industrial thermal operations is no longer just about meeting compliance mandates—it represents a major revenue opportunity. Under India's Carbon Credit Trading Scheme (CCTS), thermal power plants and heavy manufacturing units can generate monetizable carbon credits by replacing coal with certified biomass pellets.

By lowering carbon metrics, companies can enhance their global ESG status and trade verified offsets in emerging compliance carbon exchanges.

Carbon Neutrality Status of Agricultural Residues

The carbon neutrality of agricultural residues is recognized globally. Unlike fossil fuels, the CO2 released during biomass combustion is part of the short-term biogenic carbon cycle, which was recently absorbed by the crops from the atmosphere. By displacing coal, every metric ton of co-fired biomass pellets translates directly into net Scope 1 CO2 reduction.

Calculating Net Carbon Offsets and Credit Accrual

The math behind carbon credit generation is straightforward but rigorous. By multiplying the GCV displacement of coal with its respective emissions factor (approx. 2.1 tCO2 per ton of coal burned), plants can calculate their net avoided emissions. For a typical 500 MW power plant co-firing at a 5% blend, this translates to tens of thousands of carbon offset certificates annually.

Continuous Monitoring and Quality Certification

To successfully claim carbon credits, plants must document their fuel composition. Verifiers require laboratory certifications of pellet quality. Lakhdatar Green Energy provides fully traceable, certified non-torrefied biomass pellets. Our rigorous testing guarantees consistent carbon offset calculations and compliance with CPCB and Bureau of Energy Efficiency (BEE) auditing standards.

Preparing for India's Compliance Carbon Market

As the national carbon market transitions from voluntary offsets to a cap-and-trade system, carbon credits will become a highly valuable balance-sheet asset. Early integration of co-firing logistics now ensures that GENCOs and industrial buyers establish a solid track record of emissions reductions, placing them in a premium position to monetize their green transition.
