How It Works

From Field to Furnace.

Five stages. One standard: fuel that performs like an industrial input, not a compromise.

1. Residue Collection
Sourced directly from farmers across Odisha — turning what used to be burned into raw material.
2. Pelletizing & Compression
Dried, ground, and pressed into dense pellets under controlled wood-to-binder ratios.
3. Quality Verification
Every batch tested for calorific value, ash content, and moisture before it ships.
4. Bulk Delivery
Custom packaging and high-volume logistics — so your boiler never runs short.
5. Boiler Commissioning
We don’t stop at delivery. Our team calibrates feed rates, optimises combustion settings, and gets your boiler running on biomass from day one.

Q3: How do you ensure consistent fuel quality across batches?

Q5: What certifications or quality standards do your pellets meet?

Our pellets consistently meet 3,800–4,200+ kcal/kg GCV, under 10% ash content, and under 10% moisture — verified batch by batch.

Q7: How does switching to biomass affect our environmental compliance reporting?

Because biomass is a carbon-neutral fuel source, most clients see measurable improvement in emissions reporting after a full transition.

Q2: Where are your manufacturing facilities located?

Our operations are based in Tilda, Chhattisgarh, with raw material sourcing networks across Chhattisgarh and neighboring states.

Q4: Do you work directly with farmers, or through intermediaries?

We source directly from farmers, which keeps our supply chain transparent and ensures a fair, direct market for agricultural residue.

Q6: Can you support long-term supply contracts for large industrial buyers?

Yes. We structure bulk supply agreements with custom packaging and delivery logistics built around your facility's consumption rate.
