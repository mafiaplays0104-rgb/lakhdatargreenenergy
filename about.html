How It Works

From Field to Furnace.

Five stages. One standard: renewable fuel that performs identically to coal, without the emissions.

1. Agri-Residue Aggregation
Sourced directly from farmers across Central India—transforming surplus paddy and mustard stalk into valuable raw material.
2. Pelletizing & Densification
Dried, milled, and compressed into 6-8mm pellets under strict temperature and moisture controls.
3. Laboratory Verification
Every batch is rigorously tested for GCV, ash content, and moisture limits before dispatch.
4. Bulk Industrial Supply
High-volume transportation and scalable logistics—ensuring your thermal power plant maintains an uninterrupted stockpile.
5. Co-Firing Integration
We don’t stop at supply. Our engineering team assists with feed rate calibration, ensuring your plant successfully meets co-firing targets without equipment modifications.

Q1: How long has Lakhdatar Green Energy been operating?

We have been manufacturing biomass fuel since 2010, successfully integrating sustainable co-firing solutions for over 80 industrial thermal plants.

Q3: How do you ensure consistent fuel quality across batches?

Every batch undergoes strict laboratory testing for Gross Calorific Value (GCV), moisture (<10%), and ash content before leaving our manufacturing facility.

Q5: What certifications or quality standards do your pellets meet?

Our 6-8mm pellets consistently deliver 3,800–4,200+ kcal/kg GCV, under 10% ash, and under 10% moisture, strictly adhering to Ministry guidelines.

Q7: How does switching to biomass affect our environmental compliance reporting?

Because biomass is recognized as carbon-neutral, plants adopting our pellets immediately improve ESG scores and comply with mandatory co-firing regulations.

Q2: Where are your manufacturing facilities located?

Our primary operations are in Tilda, Chhattisgarh, utilizing extensive agro-residue aggregation networks across Central India to ensure consistent fuel supply.

Q4: Do you work directly with farmers, or through intermediaries?

We aggregate surplus residue directly from farmers, maintaining a transparent supply chain that prevents stubble burning and boosts rural agricultural income.

Q6: Can you support long-term supply contracts for large industrial buyers?

Absolutely. We structure Priority Sector Lending (PSL) backed bulk agreements with custom logistics designed for the massive consumption rates of power plants.

Q8: How do we start a conversation about switching our facility?

Submit a query through our 'Get A Quote' form or email info@lakhdatargreenenergy.com. We will audit your coal consumption and design a co-firing roadmap.
