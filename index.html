Q1: What is a biomass pellet, and how is it different from coal?

A biomass pellet is compressed agricultural residue — sawdust, husk, or crop waste — pressed into a dense, uniform fuel. Unlike coal, it's carbon-neutral, produces less ash, and comes from renewable, locally sourced material.

Q3: What calorific value (GCV) do your pellets deliver?

Our pellets consistently deliver 3,800–4,200+ kcal/kg, with ash content under 10% and moisture under 10% — verified batch by batch.

Q5: Do you supply in bulk for continuous industrial use?

Yes. We offer custom bulk packaging and high-volume delivery logistics designed for uninterrupted supply to industrial and commercial facilities.

Q7: What industries do you currently serve?

We supply textile mills, ceramics manufacturers, food processing plants, chemical facilities, and other industrial boiler operators across Eastern India.

Q2: Can our existing boiler run on biomass pellets without modification?

In most cases, yes. Lakhdatar Green Energy audits your current boiler setup and recommends the pellet blend and feed calibration needed for a smooth transition — often with minimal or no equipment changes.

Q4: Where do you source your raw materials?

Directly from farmers across Odisha and neighboring states, using agricultural residue that would otherwise be burned in open fields. This keeps sourcing transparent and helps prevent stubble-burning pollution at the source.

Q6: How much can we realistically reduce our coal dependency?

Clients working with us typically replace up to 97% of their coal consumption after a full transition to biomass fuel, depending on boiler type and configuration.

Q8: How do we get a quote or start the transition process?

Reach out through our Get A Quote form or contact us directly at sales@lakhdatargreenenergy.com — we'll assess your current fuel usage and recommend a transition plan.
